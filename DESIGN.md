## 1. Brand Essence

### Who Yanchan Is
**Yanchan Rajmohan** (stage name: Yanchan Produced) is a Canadian-Tamil producer, singer, mixing engineer, entrepreneur, and professional Mridangam player from Scarborough, Ontario. He is co-owner of Emtee Music Group and the architect of the viral "Mrithangam Raps" series.

### The Core Idea
**"Bridges South Indian tradition with North American hip-hop."**

Yanchan's brand is built on the fusion of two worlds that don't traditionally meet: the ancient, sacred rhythms of Carnatic classical music (played on the mridangam, kanjira, ghatam, and thavil) and the raw, contemporary pulse of hip-hop, R&B, trap, and electronic production.

### Brand Pillars
| Pillar | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Heritage** | Tamil roots, Carnatic discipline, immigrant resilience, Eelam pride |
| **Innovation** | Genre-bending fusion, fearless experimentation, "lean into the fear" |
| **Hustle** | Scarborough grind, self-made trajectory, community elevation |
| **Heart** | "Heart, Passion, Certainty" — his stated life philosophy |

### The Yanchan Difference
- Uses his legal first name as his stage name — **no alter ego**. Authenticity is non-negotiable.
- Youngest person in Canada to perform a Mridangam Arangetram (age 8).
- First artist of Eelam origin to perform at an NBA game (Raptors South Asian Heritage Night, 2024).
- His music is a love letter to Scarborough — each track is a geographic memory.

## 2. Voice & Tone

### Personality Traits
- **Warm but confident** — Humble about the journey, bold about the vision.
- **Culturally rooted, globally minded** — Speaks to diaspora kids and international audiences simultaneously.
- **Philosophical without being preachy** — Quotes like "All you need is hope" and "A life not trying is a life not lived at all."
- **Collaborative, not competitive** — Celebrates other brown artists (SVDP, Navz-47, Sid Sriram, Priya Ragu).

### Tone Guidelines
| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Social captions | Conversational, emoji-light, gratitude-heavy |
| Press / Bio | Poetic but grounded; lead with achievement, end with community |
| Merch / Product | Direct, confident, Scarborough-proud |
| Email / CRM | Personal, "from me to you," exclusive-access energy |
| Live performance | Energetic, spiritual, call-and-response with the crowd |

### Key Phrases & Mantras
- "Heart, Passion, Certainty"
- "A life not trying is a life not lived at all."
- "All you need is hope."
- "In order for things to grow you have to innovate."
- "Smooth-like-Cognac lover boy"
- "Lean into the fear."

### What to Avoid
- Never exoticize or Orientalize the Tamil/Carnatic elements.
- No "East meets West" cliché framing — it's a bridge, not a collision.
- No fake humility. Yanchan worked for every milestone.
- No separation of "classical" and "commercial" — they are the same practice.

### Primary Palette
Derived from the official 2024-05 Yanchan Produced Brand Board and validated against website assets.

| Token | Hex | Role | Usage |
|-------|-----|------|-------|
| **Void Black** | `#000000` | Primary background | Hero sections, dark mode base, video backdrops |
| **Warm Amber** | `#F58804` | Primary accent | CTAs, highlights, hover states, energetic moments |
| **Deep Rust** | `#7E2B0C` | Secondary accent | Depth, gradients, editorial moments, merch |
| **Cream Parchment** | `#F4E3C6` | Light background | Paper textures, contrast sections, lyric sheets |
| **Burnt Sienna** | `#BC4803` | Tertiary accent | Warm shadows, vintage photo tints, borders |
| **Solar Gold** | `#FFDE00` | Highlight / Energy | Tour badges, "NEW" tags, celebratory accents |

### Extended Neutrals
| Token | Hex | Role |
|-------|-----|------|
| **Charcoal** | `#1A1A1A` | Card backgrounds on black |
| **Warm Gray** | `#8A8A8A` | Muted text, captions |
| **Off-White** | `#FAF8F5` | Light section backgrounds |

### Usage Rules
- **Dark-first aesthetic:** Default to black backgrounds with warm accents. Light mode is secondary.
- **Amber is the hero:** `#F58804` is the single dominant accent. Use it sparingly for maximum impact.
- **No neon, no purple, no blue gradients.** This is a warm, analog, earth-toned brand.
- **Photo treatment:** Apply subtle warm color grading (lift shadows toward amber, compress highlights toward cream). Avoid cold/blue tints.

## 4. Typography

### Font Stack
| Role | Font | Weights | Fallbacks |
|------|------|---------|-----------|
| **Display / Headlines** | **Cehua Free** | 400, 700 | `Georgia, "Times New Roman", serif` |
| **Body / UI** | **Liberation Sans** | 400, 600, 700 | `system-ui, -apple-system, sans-serif` |
| **Monospace / Data** | **JetBrains Mono** | 400, 500 | `"Courier New", monospace` |

### Scale & Hierarchy
| Level | Size (Desktop) | Size (Mobile) | Weight | Letter-Spacing | Usage |
|-------|---------------|---------------|--------|----------------|-------|
| Hero Display | `clamp(4rem, 10vw, 8rem)` | `clamp(2.5rem, 12vw, 4rem)` | 700 | `-0.03em` | Homepage hero, tour headers |
| H1 | `clamp(3rem, 6vw, 5rem)` | `clamp(2rem, 8vw, 3rem)` | 700 | `-0.02em` | Page titles |
| H2 | `clamp(2rem, 4vw, 3rem)` | `clamp(1.5rem, 5vw, 2rem)` | 700 | `-0.01em` | Section headers |
| H3 | `1.5rem` | `1.25rem` | 600 | `0` | Subsection titles |
| Body | `1.125rem` | `1rem` | 400 | `0` | Paragraphs, bios |
| Caption | `0.875rem` | `0.8125rem` | 400 | `0.02em` | Credits, meta, dates |
| Label | `0.75rem` | `0.6875rem` | 600 | `0.08em` | Uppercase labels, nav |

### Typography Rules
- **Headlines:** Always tight tracking. Never all-caps for display type — the Cehua Free wordmark has its own rhythm.
- **Body:** Max 65 characters per line. Generous leading (`1.6–1.75`).
- **Labels:** Uppercase, wide tracking (`0.08em`), small size. Use for tour dates, "NEW," "LISTEN NOW."
- **No generic sans-serif for display.** The Cehua Free / Liberation Sans pairing is intentional — editorial meets street.

## 5. Logo System

### Primary Wordmark
The official logo is a custom wordmark reading **"yanchan produced"** in a bold, italicized, distinctive sans-serif.

- **Lockup:** Two lines — "yanchan" (larger, dominant) above "produced" (slightly smaller, offset right).
- **Color versions:** Black on light, white on dark, amber on black for special use.
- **Minimum clearspace:** Equal to the height of the "p" in "produced" on all sides.
- **Minimum size:** 120px wide for digital, 1 inch for print.

### Logo Usage Rules
- **Never distort, stretch, or rotate.**
- **Never add drop shadows, glows, or outlines.**
- **Never place the black logo on busy photographic backgrounds** without a subtle dark scrim.
- **On video/photo backgrounds:** Use the white version with a subtle `text-shadow: 0 2px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.5)`.

### Favicon / Avatar
Use the "y" glyph or the full wordmark in a square container, black background, white type.

## 6. Imagery & Photography

### Aesthetic Direction
**"Cinematic warmth. Sacred energy. Street documentation."**

Yanchan's imagery lives at the intersection of three worlds:
1. **The Stage** — Live performance, pyro, crowd energy, mridangam under stage lights.
2. **The Studio** — Intimate, warm-lit production sessions, analog gear, hands on instruments.
3. **The Street** — Scarborough landmarks, candid moments, diaspora life, Tamil cultural textures.

### Photo Treatment
- **Color grading:** Warm shadows (lift toward amber), compressed highlights (toward cream), subtle film grain.
- **Contrast:** Medium-high. Crushed blacks, lifted shadows.
- **No cold/blue tints.** The warmth is part of the brand DNA.
- **Crop & composition:** Asymmetric, editorial, generous negative space when used as backgrounds.

### Image Categories
| Category | Description | Sourcing |
|----------|-------------|----------|
| **Hero Performance** | Wide shots of Yanchan on stage with mridangam, crowd visible, dramatic lighting | Live event photography |
| **Studio Intimate** | Close-ups of hands on mridangam, beatmaking, mixing console | Behind-the-scenes |
| **Portrait Editorial** | Fashion-forward, cultural jewelry, confident gaze, neutral or textured backgrounds | Planned portrait shoots |
| **Scarborough Geography** | Neighborhood landmarks, Tamil-Canadian street life, food, community | Documentary style |
| **Album Artwork** | Abstract, painterly, warm-toned, often blending South Asian and Western visual languages | Commissioned art |

### Banned Imagery
- Stock photos of "Indian culture" (random temples, sitars, yoga).
- Overly polished, airbrushed portrait photography.
- Cold/blue-toned concert photography.
- Generic producer stereotypes (headphones on, arms crossed, fake studio).

## 7. Layout & Composition

### Spatial Philosophy
- **Generous breathing room.** Art-gallery spacing, not dense app UI.
- **Asymmetric balance.** Never default to perfect center alignment.
- **Full-bleed imagery.** Photos and videos should break container boundaries.
- **Dark-first.** Most sections sit on black or deep charcoal.

### Section Patterns
| Pattern | Usage |
|---------|-------|
| **Hero: Full-bleed video/image + left-aligned type** | Homepage, tour pages |
| **Split-screen: Image left / Content right (or vice versa)** | About, press features |
| **Editorial grid: Asymmetric 2-column with overlapping elements** | Music releases, projects |
| **Horizontal scroll: Cards peeking off-screen** | Beat store, discography |
| **Cinematic letterbox: 21:9 video/image band** | Music videos, live footage |

### Grid System
- **Desktop:** 12-column, 24px gutter, 1400px max-width.
- **Tablet:** 8-column, 20px gutter.
- **Mobile:** 4-column, 16px gutter.
- **Section padding:** `clamp(4rem, 10vw, 8rem)` vertical.

## 8. Component Design

### Buttons
| Variant | Background | Text | Border | Hover |
|---------|-----------|------|--------|-------|
| **Primary** | `#F58804` | `#000000` | none | Darken to `#BC4803`, scale 1.02 |
| **Secondary** | `transparent` | `#F58804` | `1px solid #F58804` | Fill `#F58804`, text `#000000` |
| **Ghost** | `transparent` | `#F4E3C6` | `1px solid rgba(244,227,198,0.3)` | Border brightens to `#F4E3C6` |
| **Play / Listen** | `#FFDE00` | `#000000` | none | Scale 1.05, slight bounce |

- **Border radius:** `4px` for buttons, `0px` for images.
- **Shadows:** Never colored shadows. Use subtle black shadows for depth only.

### Cards (Music Releases, Projects)
- Background: `#1A1A1A` or transparent with `1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08)` border.
- Image: Square or 4:5 ratio, no border-radius.
- Hover: Image scales 1.03, overlay appears with play icon.
- Typography: Album title (H3), Artist/features (caption), Year/label (label).

### Forms / Inputs
- Background: transparent with bottom border only (`1px solid rgba(244,227,198,0.2)`).
- Focus: Border transitions to `#F58804`.
- Labels: Uppercase, wide tracking, small size, `#8A8A8A`.
- Error: `#FF4444` with subtle shake animation.

### Navigation
- **Desktop:** Horizontal, uppercase labels, wide tracking. Active state: `#F58804` underline.
- **Mobile:** Full-screen overlay, black background, large display type for links.
- **Sticky on scroll:** Background transitions from transparent to `rgba(0,0,0,0.9)` with backdrop blur.

## 9. Motion & Animation

### Philosophy
Motion should feel **organic, percussive, and warm** — like the attack of a mridangam stroke or the decay of a reverb tail.

### Easing Curves
| Name | Value | Usage |
|------|-------|-------|
| **Mridangam Strike** | `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)` | Primary entrances, reveals |
| **Reverb Decay** | `cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)` | Subtle transitions, hovers |
| **Roll Off** | `cubic-bezier(0.7, 0, 0.84, 0)` | Exits, dismissals |

### Animation Patterns
| Pattern | Spec | Usage |
|---------|------|-------|
| **Fade-Up** | `opacity 0→1`, `translateY 30px→0`, `0.8s`, Mridangam Strike | Section entrances |
| **Stagger Cascade** | `0.1s` delay between siblings | Lists, grids, nav links |
| **Image Reveal** | Clip-path wipe from left, `1.2s` | Hero images, album art |
| **Text Slide** | `translateX -20px→0` + opacity | Headlines |
| **Pulse** | Scale `1→1.05→1`, `2s` infinite | "Now Playing" indicator, live badges |
| **Grain Overlay** | Static noise texture, `opacity: 0.03`, `pointer-events: none` | All dark backgrounds |

### Performance Rules
- Animate only `transform` and `opacity`.
- No layout-thrashing animations (width, height, top, left).
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## 10. Music & Audio UI

### The Beat Player
- **Visualizer:** Circular or waveform style, amber on black, reactive to low frequencies.
- **Controls:** Minimal — play/pause, skip, timeline, volume. No clutter.
- **Track Info:** Large album art, track title (H3), artist/features (body), BPM/key (mono label).
- **Waveform:** Amber fill on black, with played-portion in Solar Gold.

### Album Art Display
- **Grid:** Asymmetric masonry or horizontal scroll. No equal-height rows.
- **Hover:** Play button fades in + track title brightens.
- **Now Playing:** Persistent mini-player at bottom, amber accent bar showing progress.

## 13. Anti-Patterns (NEVER DO)

- **No generic "world music" framing.** Yanchan is a hip-hop producer who happens to play mridangam.
- **No cold/neon color palettes.** No purple, no electric blue, no pink gradients.
- **No stock Indian imagery.** No random sitars, temples, or Bollywood clichés.
- **No centered, symmetrical everything.** Asymmetry is part of the visual language.
- **No fake humility or corporate speak.** Voice is personal, direct, Scarborough-raised.
- **No separation of "classical" and "commercial."** They are one practice.
- **No generic sans-serif for display type.** Cehua Free is non-negotiable for headlines.
- **No emojis in primary branding.** Keep it typographic and intentional.
- **No lazy "East meets West" copy.** The brand is a bridge, not a collision.
